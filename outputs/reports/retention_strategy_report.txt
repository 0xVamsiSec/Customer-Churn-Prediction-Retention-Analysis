--- PRIORITY 1: CRITICAL-RISK CUSTOMERS (probability >= 0.75) ---
Customers            : 1365 (19.38% of base)
Average risk         : 0.8498
Historical churn     : 933 of 1365 (68.35%)
Monthly revenue pool : $109,523.90 (avg $80.24/customer)
Concentration        : 1363 month-to-month | 1188 fiber optic | 1047 electronic check | 784 tenure <= 6 months
Proposed actions:
  1. Proactive human outreach within 24-48 hours (call, not email) - highest-risk pool.
  2. Save-desk retention offers: targeted discounts or plan changes; prioritize
     month-to-month + fiber customers (largest high-risk overlap in this segment).
  3. Contract upgrade incentive to one/two-year terms (two-year contracts show the
     lowest observed churn: 2.83%).
  4. Executive/senior-agent escalation for the top probability decile.
  5. Track save rate per offer; A/B test before scaling (not yet proven to reduce churn).

--- PRIORITY 2: HIGH-RISK CUSTOMERS (probability 0.50-<0.75) ---

Monthly revenue pool : $109,332.50 (avg $73.43/customer)
Proposed actions:

  4. Monitor engagement signals; escalate to Priority 1 behavior if risk rises on re-score.

--- GOVERNANCE & CAVEATS ---
1. These are PROPOSED actions based on observed associations in historical data;
   none has been proven to reduce churn in this dataset. Validate via A/B tests.
2. Re-score the base regularly (model: models/churn_model.pkl, pipeline: run_pipeline.ps1).
3. The 0.40 operational threshold trades precision for recall; the final business
   threshold should reflect outreach cost vs expected retained revenue.
4. Training-set customers are scored in-sample; treat per-customer scores as triage
   priorities, not calibrated probabilities.
